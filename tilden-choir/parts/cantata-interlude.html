can·ta·takən-TAH-tuh, noun

A short musical play for the Christmas season, 45 minutes to an hour long. The choir sings, soloists step forward, and narrators and a few costumed characters carry the story between songs. There is no ticket and no dress code; you come, you sit, you listen. More about what a cantata is

The Tilden Community Choir is a volunteer choir of singers from Tilden and the towns around it, from any church or none at all. We have performed a cantata every Christmas since 2022 and streamed every one live. Here they are. About the choir
